# Model validation — real measurements

Bench: `tests/live/inferhub.test.ts`, run with an InferHub key over the whole corpus. Without a key
it is **skipped**: CI stays green and consumes no quota.

```bash
INFERHUB_API_KEY=… npx vitest run tests/live/inferhub.test.ts   # ~13 min (11 papers)
INFERHUB_API_KEY=… npx vitest run tests/live/enregistrer-demo.test.ts  # records the demo mode
```

Corpus: `corpus/images/` (11 PNG) + `manifeste.json` (ground truth), generated by
`scripts/generer-copies.py` — student handwriting in a handwriting font, teacher annotations in
red, and **3 degraded papers** (rotation 2–3°, uneven light, noise, slight blur, reduced contrast)
to approximate a phone photo.

> **Accepted limitation.** These are synthetic papers: they validate the prompt contract, the
> taxonomy and robustness to capture conditions. They do not replace reading real handwriting —
> protocol in [`../corpus/README.md`](../corpus/README.md).

### The single failure is instructive

Paper 8 deliberately mixes premature rounding (`100/3 = 33`), a missing unit and a wrong
conversion (`2 h 30 = 2.3 h`). The model returns `E10` where the fixture expects `E09`. Both are
defensible: this is a paper where several types coexist, and the rule "one mistake = one type"
forces a choice. **This is not a model bug, it is an ambiguity in the ground truth** — and it argues
for single-mistake fixtures when measuring accuracy.

### What degradation does not change

Rotation, uneven light, noise and slight blur cost neither the reading of annotations, nor the
typing, nor the "zero mistake" result on the clean paper. Pixelating the banner (step 2) did not
prevent reading the rest of the page either.

## Latency: the hard point, and it comes from the upstream

InferHub is a marketplace. Measurements of the evening of 11 November 2026:

| Model | Vision (corpus paper) | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| `ag/gemini-4-carbon` | 16 s | intermittent (frequent 503s) |
| `ag/claude-sonnet-4-6` | 16 s | intermittent (503) |
| `ag/gemini-3.8-flash-high` | 45–52 s | **the most reliable** |
| `ag/gemini-4-argon`, `ag/claude-sonnet-5-5`, `ag/claude-opus-4-6-thinking` | — | 503 at test time |
| families outside `ag/` (`ali/`, `oai/`, `cb/`…) | — | 402 "no provider under bid" or 403 disabled |

At a given moment, **5 of the 6 vision models were on 503**: the fallback chain is not a comfort,
it is what makes the analysis succeed.

**Reading of 12 November at 13:00 (daytime saturation)**: `ag/gemini-4-carbon` on 503,
`ag/gemini-3.8-flash-high` answers in **56.7 s for 5 output tokens**, a real 476 KB image ends in
**504 after 90 s**. Conclusion written plainly: as long as the upstream is a shared marketplace,
the brief's "photo → diagnosis < 20 s" target **is not achievable during the day**. It is in the
evening (16 s measured at 21:00). This is a model-hosting choice, not a code defect — hence the
client-side wait bounds (`DELAI_APPEL_MS`, `BUDGET_APPEL_MS`) that avoid waiting nine minutes
before saying so.

Three answers in the code:

1. **Fallback chain over the 6 `ag/` models** (`src/domain/config.ts`, `src/pipeline/llm.ts`):
   402/5xx → next model; provider 429 → 2.5 s pause then next model; proxy 429 → quota message
   and BYOK recourse.
2. **The diagnosis appears before the exercise** (`onResultatPartiel`): RNF-01 covers photo →
   diagnosis; step 6 continues in the background.
3. **Demo mode** (`src/pipeline/demo.ts`): recorded real answers, full path without network — the
   video and the public demo do not depend on the upstream.

To meet the brief's 20 s, a dedicated provider is needed (one key, one model, no bidding): that is
a configuration change (`REVISION_AMONT` + `REVISION_MODELE`), not an architecture change.

## End-to-end validation on the public demo (22:27, same day)

Real path, with no browser-side key: photo uploaded to the publicly deployed site (the earlier
deployment; current availability is documented in [`../DEMO.md`](../DEMO.md)) → Modal proxy →
InferHub → diagnosis.

Image sent: `copie-6-puissances-photo.png` (the **degraded** one: 3° rotation, uneven light, noise,
blur).

| Step | Result |
|---|---|
| Proxy calls | 8: `503, 503, 200` (extraction) then `503, 503, 503, 503, 200` (classification) |
| Diagnosis | 3 mistakes: **E06** power (0.95), **E07** root (0.98), **E99** untyped (0.75) |
| Dominant | **E06** — matches the manifest's ground truth |
| E99 | shown as "flagged, not counted in the profile" ✅ |
| Exercise | "Simplify by writing in the form a × xⁿ: (3x⁴)² × x³" — guard validated on 3 criteria |
| Profile | "50% of your mistakes: mishandled power", E06/E07 bars |
| Duration | ~40 s photo → diagnosis (mostly replayed 503s) |

Two lessons:

1. **The fallback chain is not theoretical.** Out of 8 calls, 6 failed; without it, the analysis
   would have stopped at the first 503. That is the difference between a demo that works and a demo
   that works one evening in three.
2. **A `throw` without `await` nearly broke everything.** `throw erreurDepuisReponse(…)` in an
   `async` function threw a **Promise**: no fallback (`instanceof ErreurLLM` false) and a generic
   message on screen (`instanceof Error` false). Invisible in unit tests, found only in the
   end-to-end browser test — hence the 5 fallback tests added (`tests/llm-repli.test.ts`).

### Independent replication (19:00–21:45, same day)

A second, complete and independent capture — the same four source PNGs, re-encoded separately as
JPEG, a different upstream window — gives **the same typing on all four papers**: E01 dominant
with E03 present on paper 1, E01 on papers 2 and 3, zero mistakes on paper 4. Extraction latencies
were markedly shorter in this window: 53.8 s / 30.6 s / 18.1 s / 6.7 s. The retained recordings
remain the first capture; the replication only corroborates the measurement. Expectations are
locked by `tests/demo.test.ts` ("demo corpus v2").

### What the upstream cost: 5 h of saturation

The two captures required **~60 attempts** between 17:00 and 21:45 (attempts separated by pauses;
a failed attempt writes nothing). The upstream returned 503 "no available provider" on **all 7
models** of the chain, in waves; only `zai/glm-4.6v` and `ag/gemini-3.8-flash-high` answered
intermittently (the upstream counts 514 models, but the other families are out of bid for this
account and some do not accept image input). A failed attempt writes nothing: the answer file is
written only at the end of the complete path, so **one recording = one capture**, never two.

Two things should be said plainly:

1. **The captured durations are marketplace durations, not model times**: extraction shows up to
   299 s (paper 3) because the chain replayed several dead models before one answered. Offline
   replay only waits for the capped durations (the guided tour is accelerated, a single paper
   replays real pacing): this is honest — these are actually measured durations — but they are not
   model times.
2. **An earlier probe (17:00) saw the line `g'(x) = 6x e^(−x) + (3x² − 1)e^(−x)` from paper 1 come
   back E99** (untyped) where the retained capture rates it E01 at 0.75. E99 is out of the profile:
   the dominant stays E01 in both cases, but the "one mistake = one type" rule remains at the mercy
   of a model hesitating between "lost sign" and "untyped" on a product derivative. Reported here
   rather than smoothed over.
